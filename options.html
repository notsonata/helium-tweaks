Helium Tweaks

Settings

Fullscreen video Space

Intercept video fullscreen controls, move the same tab into a temporary fullscreen Helium window, and leave a restore page in the original window. On macOS, the temporary window normally receives its own Space.

Automatic Picture-in-Picture

Enter PiP when you switch away from a tab with a playing video. Fullscreen video transfers and restore-placeholder activations are excluded automatically.

Disable the separate Better Auto PiP extension before enabling this. Extensions cannot reliably cancel another extension's PiP request.

Bookmarks sidebar

The toolbar button opens this settings page. Use the keyboard shortcut to open and focus the bookmark sidebar on the current tab.
